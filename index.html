Three pier quotes, three systems. Get an engineer plan before you buy the one the seller installs.

Houston foundation jobs mix a free seller inspection with a five-to-thirty-thousand-dollar pier package. Homeowners keep comparing totals that are not the same system. Use this list before you sign.

  • Ask whether this visit is a seller inspection or a licensed PE plan.
  • Ask which system they bid: pressed pilings, steel push, helical, or bell-bottom — and what soil fact drove it.
  • Ask for pier count, depth or refusal rule, and lift vs stabilize-only.
  • Ask whether extra depth is a change order after they start.
  • Call a shop that publishes a phone on its own website.
