Common questions

Should I get an engineer before I pick a pier company?

Sellers often inspect for free and recommend the system they install. An independent PE plan is a different document. Ask who stamps the drawing.

Is one pier type the Houston standard?

No. Pressed pilings are common. Steel push, helical, and drilled bell-bottom show up on other bids. Ask why that system fits your soil and load.

What should be on the written number?

Pier count, target depth or refusal rule, lift versus stabilize-only, and what extra depth costs after they start.
